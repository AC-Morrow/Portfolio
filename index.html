Case Study

From hypothesis to endurance creative

How Reddit comment mining became DRMTLGY's lowest-CPA concept and how one post-production swap nearly doubled the thumbstop rate.

DRMTLGY Growth Factor Serum
The Forehead Lines Campaign
The Challenge

DRMTLGY's anti-aging creative was over-indexed on generic wrinkle messaging. The smoker lines concept had already proven that hyper-specific placement targeting outperforms broad anti-aging copy. The question was: what's the next high-signal pain point?

My Approach

Comment and review mining across DRMTLGY's existing ads and Reddit (r/30PlusSkinCare, r/SkincareAddiction) surfaced forehead line mentions at 2:1 vs smoker lines. I briefed a UGC Yapper format with three hook variations guarantee-first, insecurity angle, social proof testing which emotional lever converted hardest.

Iteration Logic

Hook A proved the concept. But the thumbstop rate (22%) was lagging behind what the body was delivering. So I isolated the one variable: swap the hook visual only, keep everything downstream identical. A creator clip of a highlighter marker tracing forehead lines delivered the visual curiosity needed to stop the scroll.

Methodology

The system behind every brief

Creative strategy isn't ideation. It's a research-driven system where the client's CPA and revenue goals are the northstar and every brief is built to serve that number.

01
Research & Signal Mining
Comment mining across existing ads, Reddit communities, Amazon reviews, and competitor creative. I find the language real customers use not the language brands think they use. That language becomes the hook.
02
Hypothesis-Led Briefs
Every brief starts with a testable hypothesis: "If we speak to X pain point using Y format, we expect Z improvement in metric." The creative follows the hypothesis not the other way around.
03
Isolation Testing
When a concept proves itself, I isolate one variable at a time hook visual, hook copy, format, VO to understand what's doing the work. This prevents throwing out winners and builds a scalable creative library.
04
CPA as Northstar
CTR tells me if the creative is compelling. Hold rate tells me if the story holds. CPA tells me if it works. Every creative decision traces back to the client's efficiency and revenue goals.
05
Endurance Architecture
I build creative systems that compound, not one-hit campaigns. A winning concept spawns a testing roadmap: format variations, hook iterations, persona expansions.
06
AI-Augmented Research
I use AI tools to accelerate signal mining, script iteration, and competitive analysis without losing the strategic judgment that makes briefs actually perform.

Brief Tiers

Tier 1 Low Lift
Hook & Copy Iterations
Post-production swaps on existing winning creative. New hook visual, new opening copy same body, same VO, same CTA. Fast to produce, high signal, low risk.
2–3 day turnaround No new shoot Existing footage
Tier 2 Mid Lift
New Concept, Proven Format
New script and hook, same proven UGC or static format. Tests new messaging angles without rebuilding the whole playbook. Requires creator briefing and light production.
1–2 week turnaround Creator briefing New script
Tier 3 High Lift
Full Production Concept
New concept, new format, new shoot. Reserved for validated insights that warrant real investment a new persona, a new channel, or a signal strong enough to justify full production cost.
3–4 week turnaround Full production New format
